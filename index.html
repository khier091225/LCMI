Get to know LCMI

Laguna Carparts Mfg., Inc. (LCMI) was established in October 2004 with the goal of supporting the Original Equipment supply chain of the local automotive industry. The company produces automotive exhaust systems along with metal and plastic component parts that are essential to vehicle performance and durability.

LCMI also fulfills the component requirements of its sister companies, namely Almazora Motors Corporation, Almazora Fujino Metal Industries, and DK dwa Automotive Parts (Phils.) Corporation, ensuring a reliable and well-coordinated supply network.

Since its founding, LCMI has continuously invested in upgrading its facilities and equipment to keep pace with the standards of its partner companies. With over a decade of dedicated service, the company has earned a reputation for reliability and consistency, achieving one hundred percent performance in both delivery, quality and safety requirements.
