About LCMI.

Automotive manufacturing in the Philippines since October 2004.

Company profile & leadership

Laguna Carparts Mfg., Inc. (LCMI) was established in October 2004 with the goal of supporting the Original Equipment supply chain of the local automotive industry. The company produces automotive exhaust systems along with metal and plastic component parts that are essential to vehicle performance and durability.

Company profile & leadership

LCMI also fulfills the component requirements of its sister companies, namely Almazora Motors Corporation, Almazora Fujino Metal Industries, and DK dwa Automotive Parts (Phils.) Corporation, ensuring a reliable and well-coordinated supply network.

Since its founding, LCMI has continuously invested in upgrading its facilities and equipment to keep pace with the standards of its partner companies. With over a decade of dedicated service, the company has earned a reputation for reliability and consistency, achieving one hundred percent performance in both delivery, quality and safety requirements.

Quality & environment policy

As we conduct our business, Laguna Carparts Mfg., Inc, an OEM manufacturer of Exhaust systems, metal, and plastic parts for the automotive, appliance, and electronics industry, is committed to satisfying our customers and protecting and improving our environment.

The highest priority in our job is understanding our customer's quality and environmental requirements, needs, and expectations.

To satisfy these requirements and expectations, we are guided by the principles of

  • Excellent Quality
  • On-time Delivery
  • On-time development
  • Lowest cost possible
  • Compliance with all legal and regulatory requirements
  • Compliance with customer-specific requirements
  • Inclusion of environmental and safety factors during APQP
  • A good relationship with all our interested parties

Through continuous education and training, we are building the company's reputation and the morale of the employees.

We treat challenges as opportunities for the growth of our company and our people.

We are driven to provide more than what our customers require.

At Laguna Carparts, Customers' Delight is Assured.
